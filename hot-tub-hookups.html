Can you review the electrical setup before my tub arrives?

Yes. Share the model and manufacturer’s installation requirements, along with photos of the panel and planned location, so we can discuss the next step before delivery.

Will I need a panel upgrade?

That depends on your existing electrical system and the spa’s requirements. An assessment determines whether the available capacity and equipment are suitable.

Can you reuse an existing hot tub connection?

An existing connection needs to be evaluated against the replacement spa’s requirements and its current condition. A previous hookup does not automatically mean it is suitable for a new model.

How long will the electrical work take?

Timing depends on the wiring route, site access, panel work, and any permit or inspection scheduling. We discuss the expected schedule after reviewing the scope.

Does this include moving or setting the hot tub?

This service page covers the electrical hookup. Delivery, placement, structural work, landscaping, and any other trades should be confirmed separately in the written project scope.

What if my hot tub is sold as plug-and-play?

Send the model information and installation manual. We can review the intended power supply and discuss whether electrical work is needed for your setup.

Do you install the AC unit or furnace itself?

This service covers electrical work. Equipment installation, refrigerant work, ductwork, and gas work should be arranged with the appropriate HVAC contractor.

Can the existing circuit be reused?

The existing circuit must be evaluated against the replacement equipment requirements and its condition before reuse is confirmed.

Can you coordinate with my HVAC company?

Yes. Share their contact details, equipment specifications, and installation schedule so the electrical scope and timing can be discussed.

Can you quote before the equipment is selected?

We can discuss the project, but final equipment specifications may be needed to define and price the electrical work accurately.

Does every replacement require new wiring?

Not necessarily. The required work depends on the existing installation and the new equipment’s requirements.
